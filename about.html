Our Story

From a single warehouse to a regional supply network

TRADEXPO.INC started in Yakima, Washington with a simple mandate: get schools, hospitals, and growing businesses the electronics they need, in the volume they need, without the delays of consumer retail channels.

Since 2006, we've expanded our catalog to cover eight major equipment categories and built direct relationships with manufacturers, which lets us pass volume pricing straight to institutional buyers.

Today, our team manages sourcing, bulk logistics, and account support for clients across dozens of cities — but we've kept the same close, responsive service that earned our first customers.

Our Mission

To be the single, reliable source institutions turn to for bulk electronics — combining fair pricing, deep inventory, and logistics that respect deadlines that matter, like a hospital wing opening or a school year starting on time.

Our Vision

A supply chain where every school, clinic, and business — regardless of size — has direct access to enterprise-grade electronics and the account support usually reserved for the largest buyers.
